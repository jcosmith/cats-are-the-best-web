# Cats Are the Best: Plan & Resources

## 1. Plan

**Goal:** A 20-slide HTML presentation (`cats-are-the-best.html`) on cats, mostly about **how to train your cat**. It should be grounded in reputable sources: shelters and humane societies, veterinary bodies, and peer-reviewed studies.

**Audience:** Cat owners and would-be owners. No training experience is assumed.

**Format:** A single self-contained HTML file. Keyboard, click and swipe navigation, a progress bar and slide counter, and light/dark support. Photos are freely licensed Wikimedia Commons images stored locally in `./images/`, so the deck works offline (fonts fall back to system fonts).

## 2. Resources

### Science & history
1. **Vigne, J.-D. et al. (2004).** "Early taming of the cat in Cyprus." *Science* 304:259.
   - 9,500-year-old human–cat burial at Shillourokambos, Cyprus, about 4,000 years older than Egyptian cat art.
   - https://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/article/oldest-known-pet-cat-9500-year-old-burial-found-on-cyprus
   - https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2004/04/040409092827.htm
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shillourokambos
2. **Driscoll, C. A. et al. (2007).** "The Near Eastern origin of cat domestication." *Science* 317:519–523.
   - Domestic cats descend from the Near Eastern wildcat *Felis silvestris lybica*.
3. **Vitale Shreve, K. R., Mehrkam, L. R. & Udell, M. A. R. (2017).** "Social interaction, food, scent or toys? A formal assessment of domestic pet and shelter cat preferences." *Behavioural Processes* 141:322–328. doi:10.1016/j.beproc.2017.03.016
   - 50% (19/38) most preferred human social interaction; 37% food; 11% toys; 2% scent.
   - Big individual differences, which is why you should find *your* cat's favorite reward.
   - https://phys.org/news/2017-03-cats-humans-thought.html
4. **Saito, A., Shinozuka, K., Ito, Y. & Hasegawa, T. (2019).** "Domestic cats (*Felis catus*) discriminate their names from other words." *Scientific Reports* 9:5394.
   - Four experiments, 16–34 cats each; cats habituated to other words, then reacted to their own name.
   - Cats probably link their name with outcomes (food, play, vet), which is useful for recall training.
   - https://www.boston.com/news/animals/2019/04/04/cats-react-name/
5. **Dantas, L. M. S., Delgado, M. M., Johnson, I. & Buffington, C. T. (2016).** "Food puzzles for cats: Feeding for physical and emotional wellbeing." *J Feline Med Surg* 18(9):723–732.
   - Case studies: puzzles helped with weight loss, anxiety and house-soiling.
   - https://czaw.org/resources/food-puzzles-for-cats-feeding-for-physical-and-emotional-wellbeing/
   - UC Davis follow-up survey (3,192 owners): only 30% use food puzzles, and 52% have never tried one. https://www.ucdavis.edu/health/news/study-finds-nearly-third-cat-owners-use-food-puzzles

### Veterinary guidance
6. **AAFP/ISFM (2022).** *Cat Friendly Veterinary Interaction Guidelines: Approach and Handling Techniques.* *J Feline Med Surg* 24:1093–1132.
   - Replaced the 2011 Feline-Friendly Handling Guidelines. Emphasises minimal handling, the cat's **choice and control**, and caregiver carrier training.
   - https://catvets.com/interactions
   - https://www.veterinarypracticenews.com/aafp-and-isfm-release-feline-friendly-handling-guidelines/
7. **International Cat Care (iCatCare).** Free handling and carrier-training video series made with Ceva, from the International Cat Day 2021 #BeCatCurious campaign.
   - Poll: 95% of owners wanted more training advice; over half struggled to get their cat into a carrier.
   - Dr Sarah Ellis: anyone can train their cat.
   - https://bsava.com/article/promoting-cat-training-to-reduce-veterinary-visit-stress-a-key-focus-of-international-cat-day-2021/
   - https://icatcare.org
8. **VCA Hospitals.** "How to read your cat's body language."
   - https://vcahospitals.com/pediatric/kitten/behavior-training/how-to-read-your-cats-body-language
9. **Cornell Feline Health Center.** "Feline behavior problems: destructive behavior."
   - https://vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/feline-behavior-problems-destructive-behavior

### Shelter & humane-society training guides
10. **Humane Colorado.** Cat clicker training: click then treat ×5 to "charge" the clicker. https://humanecolorado.org/resources/cat-clicker-training/
11. **Humane Society of Southern Colorado.** *Cat Clicker Training Basics* (PDF): the click as a "bridge"; be generous with rewards. https://humanesocietysoco.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/HSSC_Cat_Clicker_Training_Basics.pdf
12. **SPCA Cincinnati.** *Cat Clicker Training* (PDF). https://www.spcacincinnati.org/media/adopt/Cat%20Training/CatClickerTraining-149.pdf
13. **Kinship.** "A step-by-step guide on how to clicker train your cat": targeting with a chopstick or spatula, and guiding the cat to carrier or bed without handling. https://kinship.com/cat-behavior/clicker-training-for-cats
14. **Cats.com.** "How to clicker train a cat" and "How to train a cat to walk on a leash." https://cats.com/how-to-train-a-cat-to-walk-on-a-leash
15. **ASPCA.** "Scratching behavior in cats": several surface types and orientations, never force paws onto the post, the ASPCA opposes declawing. https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/general-pet-care/scratching-behavior-cats
16. **ASPCA.** "Common cat behavior issues." https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/cat-care/common-cat-behavior-issues
17. **North Shore Animal League America.** "Important cat training tips": posts near sleeping spots, house-soiling → vet first. https://www.animalleague.org/blog/tips/pet-adoption/important-cat-training-tips/
18. **HSUS / Dumb Friends League handout (via Asheville Humane).** *Destructive Scratching*: punishment after the fact doesn't work, and cats associate it with the person. https://www.ashevillehumane.org/wp-content/uploads/Destructive-Scratching.pdf
19. **San Francisco SPCA.** *Correcting your cat's behavior* (PDF). https://www.sfspca.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/cat_correcting-your-cats-behavior.pdf
20. **Toronto Humane Society.** *Harness Training Your Cat* (PDF): many cats flop at first; train before meals. https://www.torontohumanesociety.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Harness-Training-Your-Cat.pdf
21. **Wisconsin Humane Society.** "Leash training your cat": harness, not collar. https://www.wihumane.org/resource/leash-training-your-cat/
22. **Ontario SPCA (2026).** "How to teach a cat recall." https://ontariospca.ca/blog/how-to-teach-a-cat-recall/
23. **Your Cat magazine.** "Teach your cat to come when called": variable rewards once learned. https://www.yourcat.co.uk/cat-advice/training-tips-teach-your-cat-to-come-when-called
24. **Joyful Pets.** Cat behavior training: litter box setup and enzymatic cleaners. https://www.joyfulpets.com/cat-behavior-training/

### Background reading (books)
- John Bradshaw, *Cat Sense* (2013): the science of cat behavior and domestication.
- Karen Pryor, *Getting Started: Clicker Training for Cats* (2001).
- Sarah Ellis & John Bradshaw, *The Trainable Cat* (2016): an iCatCare-aligned training guide.

## 3. Notes on source quality
- Peer-reviewed studies (Vigne, Driscoll, Vitale, Saito, Dantas) are cited for factual claims and statistics.
- Training methods come from humane societies, the ASPCA, iCatCare and AAFP/ISFM. They agree on the core points: **positive reinforcement, short sessions, the cat's choice, no punishment**.
- Low-quality or SEO-generated pages found in search were excluded.
